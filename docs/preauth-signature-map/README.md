# Pre-auth forms: where the doctor signs, stamps and the hospital seals

Reviewed all 42 insurer / TPA pre-authorisation forms in `public/preauth/`. Every form was read for "Doctor's Signature", "Hospital Seal" and similar captions; three forms that are only pictures (Geninus, ICICI Lombard, Star Health) and two whose last page has no text layer (Health India, Heritage) were read by eye.

Open **`signature-and-stamp-map.pdf`** for the marked pages: every place is outlined and labelled (red = doctor signature + stamp, blue = hospital seal), one or two pages per insurer, with the insurer name and page number in the red band at the top.

## Forms that need the doctor's signature and stamp in more than one place

| Insurer | Places | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Cholamandalam MS General Insurance | 2 | Page 2 (end of the treating-doctor section, "Signature of Treating Doctor") and page 4 (Hospital Declaration, "Doctor's Signature") |
| Generali Central Insurance | 2 | Page 2 twice: "Signature of Doctor" in the declaration at the top, and "Doctor's Signature" in the Hospital Declaration at the bottom |
| IFFCO Tokio General Insurance | 2 | Page 2 twice: "Signature of treating doctor" next to the seal and patient signature, and "Doctor's Signature" in the Hospital Declaration |
| Royal Sundaram General Insurance | 2 | Page 2 ("Hospital Seal ... and attending Physician's Signature") and page 3 (Hospital Declaration, "Doctor's Signature") |

Every other form asks for the doctor's signature once, in the Hospital Declaration on the last page. All forms ask for the **hospital seal** in two places — beside the patient's signature at the end of the doctor section, and beside the doctor's signature in the Hospital Declaration — except the ones noted below.

## Notes

- Forms with a single hospital seal place: Indusind (Reliance), Medi Assist, SBI General, ICICI Lombard, Star Health.
- Geninus, ICICI Lombard and Star Health are scanned pictures with no text layer; their places were read from the page image. Geninus and Star Health have no exact-form template in the app, so the app cannot place pictures on them yet.
- The marked boxes show where the signing space is; on a few crowded forms (Generali Central, Acko) the space is small and the box overlaps printed text.
